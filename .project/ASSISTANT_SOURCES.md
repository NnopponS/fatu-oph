## Prepared answers and freshness requirements

Every record carries `id`, `title`, `text`, optional `url`, `kind`, optional `academicYear`, `verifiedAt`, `keywords`, `authority`, and `confidence`. Use 2570 records for current eligibility; historical 2569 records must explicitly announce their year. Missing thresholds, new announcements, individual exemptions and recommendations beyond verified evidence should be escalated to admissions staff. Linking social media does not mean the assistant monitors all posts or messages. Refresh these sources when new round notices appear.
